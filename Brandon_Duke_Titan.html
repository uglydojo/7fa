About $500,000 a month gross, not counting ad spend. Ten attorney firms on retainer across six states, thirty to forty buyers across five verticals. Your own target is $1 million months a year from now.

My read on it

Two things are going to decide whether you hit $1 million months, and neither one is a traffic problem.

The first is hiring. The bottleneck is finding people who can media buy on your behalf. That's a people problem at your scale, and it's the single most common thing in that room, which is why the coaching is worth more to you than any playbook.

The second is separate, and it's the one I'd watch. Motor vehicle accident lead gen is saturated now, and you've already had to cut traffic sources to protect client CPAs. You don't have an authority platform yet, no podcast, no channel, just the occasional LinkedIn post. The agencies that hold margin in a saturated vertical are the ones buyers come looking for. Jay Berkowitz got to Titan level in your exact niche on a monthly webinar and Google. That's the lever I'd pull with you first, and it's a conversation, not a product.

That's my read and you're welcome to tell me I've got it wrong.
