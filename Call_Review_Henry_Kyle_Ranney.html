What Henry Did Right

  1. Opened on Josh's books. "How long have you been following Josh?" (0:17) got Kyle saying he'd read both. Offering the audiobook (0:46) was reciprocity in minute one.
  2. Used the setter notes. Read back the booking info and asked "Did I cover everything?" (2:36). Kyle confirmed and added the real issue, no niche (3:24). Problem on the table in under 3 minutes.
  3. Let Kyle find his own niche. "What makes you scared about that?" (3:46) took Kyle to "I guess I'm kind of already niched in it" (4:45). Kyle did that work, not Henry.
  4. Cleared up the niche rule in one line. "The change in services, that's perfectly fine. It's about the industry" (5:14).
  5. Named the negative emotions. "It seems like mainly it's systems that are not in your control" (16:25) got "Yep, basically. Got me." Then "Those months must be tough" (18:44).
  6. Ran a real why chain. "Why 50K?" (17:53) then "What about you on a personal level?" (18:49) got "I'm working all the freaking time" and "I don't like being alone." That's the emotional core of the call.
  7. Played the goal back in Kyle's words. "So a 50K a month goal, building a team... having more time. Freedom..." (20:30). Kyle: "Yeah, that's it, man." The best moment of the call.
  8. Asked about past programs. "Have you ever gone for coaching and mentorship before?" (21:16) brought out the YouGurus burn.
  9. Got the time commitment before talking about results. "Can you set aside at least five to ten hours a week?" (32:26) got "Oh, definitely."
  10. Held after the price. Kyle spoke first: "Okay, that's not bad." (31:31)
  11. Found the real objection. "Do you think there's anything else that might stop you from moving forward?" (35:06) got "just $1,500 a month." Then answered with Kyle's own words: "I thought you said this was one of the good months... That's when you take action" (35:23).
  12. Tried to get the wife on. "Is she home right now?" (36:22). Right instinct.
  13. Locked a decision date. "By when can you give me any decision?" (37:02) got "end of the week," with the $997 first month tied to Friday 5 PM ET (37:35).

Where It Slipped

  • Normalizing the fear. Kyle: "it seems very scary." Henry: "I'm sure you hear that a lot. It is a big leap" (3:46).
  • Joking past the pain. Right after Kyle admitted he's winging it: "I think that's what we call spray and pray" (16:43).
  • Answering the freedom desire with more work. Kyle: "I'm working all the freaking time." Henry: "the initial part of this is where you have to put in even more work than you are putting in right now" (19:16).
  • Joking about pressure at the decision point. "This is where I want to start applying pressure... I'm just joking" (36:41).

The Fatal Mistake

At 34:46 Kyle said: "I think that's a really good value. I can definitely afford it... what would the next steps be? You just see a payment link kind of thing?" That's a buyer asking how to pay. Henry answered with a question, accepted "talk to the missus," and sent an email. The wife door had been open since 22:27 and should have been closed before the price. At 34:46 the line was:

"Yeah, I'll open it right now. Before I do, you mentioned your wife. Is she part of this decision, or is it the Miami trip you want to run by her? Because the membership starts today either way, and Miami is optional with virtual access."

Top 3 Coaching Priorities

1. When they ask how to pay, take the payment.

At 34:46 Kyle asked for the link and got a question back.

"Most people use Visa, Mastercard or Amex. Which one do you want to use today? I'll type it in while we're on."

2. Close the spouse door when it opens.

At 22:27 Kyle mentioned his wife and the reply was event dates.

"You mentioned your wife. Outside of the trip, is she part of the decision to invest in the business, or is that yours?"

3. Temp check before and after price.

Both were skipped, even with "that's not bad" handed over.

"On a scale of 1 to 10, with everything included and the investment, where are you?"  If 8: "Why not a 4?"

Accuracy Notes for the Team

  • Price: Accelerate is $1,497/mo, not "$1,500" (31:25).
  • Guarantee: it's the 60-day perfect fit guarantee. State it once. Avoid "full 100% refund, no questions, no conditions" (31:39).
  • Results: hedge outcome numbers with "potentially." "Majority of our members get at least 20 to 25K a month... within the first three months" (32:48) reads as a promise.
