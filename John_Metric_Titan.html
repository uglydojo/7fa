01Your Five Questions

In the order you asked them.

1. Is access to coaches unlimited?

Yes. You get a coach assigned to you for Titans, and beyond that there is no cap on how many conversations you or your team book. The only real limit is what the coaches' calendars allow.

What I said on the call, word for word: "the fee that you're ever going to pay is $3,000 a month. That's it. You can have as many conversations as their calendars allow for you to book them. And we don't really put caps on it."

Every coach is operating an agency or has exited one. None of them are career coaches.

2. Can your President attend the retreats virtually, and do you get a recording?

Yes to both, and I answered this on our call so your partners can hear it rather than take my word for it here: "If you can't make it in person, we broadcast the room live while it's happening, and we record everything so you get access to everything."

For November specifically, the Titan days on the 11th and 12th run live in Miami and livestreamed.

One thing worth knowing rather than discovering: confidentiality is a condition of membership. Numbers, decks and what gets said in the room stay inside the group. That is the reason the room is worth sitting in, and it is the reason I would rather your President be on the live stream as a named seat than have a recording circulate.

Your President may also qualify to be in the room in person rather than on the stream. Partners, a COO, a head of operations and an EOS integrator all sit in. Tell me which of those fits him and I will confirm it.

3. Cohort and virtual cadence: can your President attend alongside other A players?

Your membership covers up to five key players in your company, and you decide who they are. That is not just you. On the call: "that includes access to your A players. This membership is not just for you. It's for you and your team."

Those five seats cover the virtual coaching sessions and the subject-matter sessions, and your team is welcome in those. So yes to your VP Revenue, VP Finance, BDM and AED sitting in on the coaching and training cadence.

One piece of arithmetic worth naming before your partners spot it. You and your President plus those four roles is six people against five seats. On our call I told you: "access to up to five key players in your company. If you need more access, just let us know." That stands. Send me the six names and titles and I will get the sixth seat sorted rather than making you choose which of your leaders gets left out.

The cohort itself is the owners' room. There are about fifty Titans, and we put people into cohorts of six to eight that mastermind regularly. Whether your President can sit in on the cohort call specifically is the one piece I am confirming rather than guessing, for the same confidentiality reason above. I will have that answer for you.

4. What meals are covered?

For the Miami Intensive, sessions and meals run on the hotel property. Specifically:

  • Sunday evening welcome cocktail reception, hosted
  • Tuesday 5:30pm all-member cocktail mixer, then the 6:30pm recognition dinner, hosted
  • Lunches are on your own. The agenda carries a restaurant list

Titans gatherings include a welcome dinner and a closing dinner. Travel and lodging are yours to cover.

5. The website mentions St. Louis. Same series or different?

Different, and that is a fair thing to be confused by.

The Intensives are in Miami. Three a year, March, July and November. Your Titan days sit inside those, including November 11 and 12.

Separately there is a Titans gatherings series, and our site lists St. Louis in August 2026 as one of them. I am not going to quote you a 2027 gathering calendar I have not personally confirmed, so I am getting the definitive one from Yesenia and sending it to you rather than guessing in writing.

02What This Looks Like for Metric

Your numbers, not a brochure.

Metric is at roughly $6M CAD, about $4.5M USD, with 95% of it on recurring contracts. The goal you named is $10M USD by 2030, and the plan you laid out is to grow through 2027, 2028 and 2029 and put the company on the market in 2030.

So the question is not whether Titans has useful content. It is whether the room moves a $4.5M company to $10M in four years while making it more sellable at the end of it.

The cohort is the part that answers your third request

You asked me to connect you with three Titan founders at the $5M to $10M level for a peer-to-peer validation call. I understand why. On the call you put it this way: "if I could be in a group of six to eight people, six to eight agencies that are in that four to 10 range, then obviously I'm talking to people that are going through the same stuff that I am."

Here is what I committed to you on that call, and it is a stronger version of what you are asking for:

  • "we'll make sure when we have a new Titan member that when we do assign a cohort to you, that you are the least paid in the cohort"
  • "So everybody there, it's going to be at least 5 million plus"

Three validation calls would give your partners three conversations. The cohort gives you six to eight agencies at or above your size, permanently, where you are the smallest one in the room by design. That is the thing I would want if I were building your case for your partners.

If after reading that you still want three individual calls before you decide, say so and I will go ask members directly. I am not going to volunteer other members' time on this page as though it were mine to give.

My read, and it is a read, not a fact

It sounds like the real driver here is not the next million in revenue. You have a board of advisors with people who have exited, and the gap you named was that they are "not people that have done it lately and had niche as well too."

If you are selling in 2030, the people who make that number bigger are operators who sold a niche agency recently, in a market that looks like today's. That is a narrower room than a general mastermind, and it is the specific thing your current group cannot give you, in your own words: "You're kind of talking apples when they're talking oranges."
